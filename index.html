Data & privacy

18 fictional employees across six departments. Four synthetic quarterly observations per employee. All records are bundled in the page. Demo role controls limit displayed data but do not provide real security. Check-ins and goals are stored locally and should never contain real personal information.

Pulse AI limitations

Pulse uses a local intent-matching and recommendation engine, not a hosted large language model. It retrieves current demo metrics, calculates gaps and suggests coaching actions. It cannot reliably answer unrelated questions, predict attrition, authorize HR actions or determine promotions. For production: secure role-based access, audit logs, consent, validated models and human review.
